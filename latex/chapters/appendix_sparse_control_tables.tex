\subsection{Compact experimental details}
\label{app:sparse-control-method-details}

The two deterministic gate decoders are defined in
Section~\ref{subsec:sparse-control-gate}. The gate-plus-budget diagnostic also
separates the gate and non-idle entropy terms:
\[
  H_i=\alpha_{gate}H(g_i)+\alpha_{nonidle}H(b_i),
\]
instead of using the coupled form
$H_i=H(g_i)+P(g_i=1)H(b_i)$. This avoids rewarding intervention merely because
a larger intervention probability exposes more non-idle entropy.
\\
\\
The fixed-penalty experiment uses flat and gated actors,
$\lambda_{fixed}\in\{0,0.001,0.003,0.01\}$, and seeds
$\{0,1,2\}$, giving 24 runs. The
tested conditions are flat local-safe budgets
$d\in\{0.10,0.20,0.35\}$, a gated local-safe budget with $d=0.20$, and a flat
plain non-idle budget with $d=0.20$.
\\
\\
For the teacher-student diagnostic, the teacher is the MAPPO policy evaluated
with the local rho heuristic. The student is trained to reproduce the
teacher's executed actions and is evaluated without the heuristic. The two
reported students use balanced non-idle fractions of $0.50$ and $0.20$, with
non-idle loss weights of $5$ and $3$, and auxiliary weights of $0.50$ and
$0.25$, respectively.
