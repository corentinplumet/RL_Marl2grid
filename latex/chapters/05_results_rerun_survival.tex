\chapter{Preliminary Results}

\section{Rerun Episodic Survival Ablations}

This section summarizes the rerun survival plots generated from the episodic
survival rerun notebook.
The metric is test episodic survival in percent. Thick lines show the
seed-aggregated mean curve, faint lines show individual seed curves, and shaded
regions show the variability band used in the notebook plots.

\subsection{Result Summary}

The reruns suggest three main trends. First, the strongest GINE rerun is the
light A0-style GINE with an MLP critic and optimized critic updates
(\texttt{best\_14}), which reaches a final survival of about
\(95.7\%\). Second, the A0 MLP baseline is robust: \texttt{rerun\_a0\_opt},
\texttt{rerun\_a0\_nonopt}, and the smaller \texttt{rerun\_a01} variant all
finish around \(93\%\) survival, while disabling reward normalization
(\texttt{rerun\_a02}) ends lower. Third, the initial do-nothing bias has a large
but non-monotonic effect in these reruns: \texttt{rerun\_bias\_05} performs
poorly, while \texttt{rerun\_bias\_07} reaches nearly perfect final survival.

\subsection{GINE best\_00 Versus best\_10--best\_14}

Figure~\ref{fig:rerun-gine-best00} compares the historical
\texttt{best\_00} GINE baseline against five controlled reruns. Removing the
initial do-nothing bias alone (\texttt{best\_11}) does not improve the final
curve. The optimized-critic variants are more competitive, and the strongest
curve is \texttt{best\_14}, which combines a lighter GINE actor, MLP critic,
A0-style entropy and initialization choices, no flat concatenation, and
optimized critic updates.

\subsection{A0 Initial Do-Nothing Bias}

Figure~\ref{fig:rerun-a0-bias} isolates the initial do-nothing bias. The
\texttt{rerun\_bias\_05} curve remains unstable and substantially below the
zero-bias baseline, whereas \texttt{rerun\_bias\_07} eventually reaches the
highest final survival in this comparison. The conclusion is therefore not that
larger bias is always better, but that the initialization prior strongly shapes
exploration and should be treated as a first-order ablation variable.

\subsection{Ablation Takeaway}

Overall, these reruns indicate that the strongest current direction is not a
single isolated modification, but the combination of stable A0-style training
choices with architectures that keep the critic and graph encoder simple enough
to optimize reliably. Reward normalization and initialization bias both have
large effects on survival, while optimized critic updates and lighter GINE/MLP
critic variants can improve the final GINE rerun performance.
