\chapter{Experiments}
\label{ch:experiments}

This chapter defines the current staged graph-screening campaign. A run denotes
one training configuration with one random seed. The campaign contains 44
configured runs across Stages 1, 1b, 1c, 1d, and 1e. This chapter records what
is varied and what is held fixed; measured outcomes are reported separately in
Chapter~\ref{ch:experimental-results}.

\section{Common Training Protocol}
\label{sec:screening-common-protocol}

Unless a stage states otherwise, every run uses the paired configuration in
Table~\ref{tab:screening-common-settings}. The purpose of this common protocol
is to isolate changes to the graph input or graph structure.

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \small
  \caption{Settings shared by the current graph-screening runs.}
  \label{tab:screening-common-settings}
  \setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
  \begin{tabular}{L{0.25\textwidth}L{0.65\textwidth}}
    \toprule
    Component & Fixed setting \\
    \midrule
    Environment & \texttt{bus14}, difficulty 0, topology actions, 80/20 train/test chronic split with split seed 0 \\
    Actor & One GINE encoder shared by all agents; two message-passing layers; 128 hidden and output features; LayerNorm; two 128-unit action-head layers \\
    Critic & Centralized MLP with three 256-unit hidden layers \\
    Training budget & \(8{,}000{,}000\) environment steps, 72 parallel environments, 576 rollout steps \\
    MAPPO update & 10 epochs, 16 minibatches, actor and critic learning rates \(10^{-4}\) with linear annealing, \(\gamma=0.9\), \(\lambda=0.95\) \\
    Evaluation & Deterministic evaluation every 82,944 environment steps; 10 episodes on each evaluated split \\
    Graph defaults & Bidirectional relations, mean readout, no same-substation edges, no substation-summary nodes, no virtual-node readout \\
    Other input settings & No global node-identifier embeddings and no concatenated flat observation in the actor \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The graph preprocessing labels used below are
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    \texttt{n0\_none} &: \text{no graph-feature preprocessing},\\
    \texttt{n1\_physical} &: \text{deterministic physical scaling only},\\
    \texttt{n2\_running} &: \text{running standardization only},\\
    \texttt{n3\_both} &: \text{physical scaling followed by running standardization}.
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:screening-normalization-labels}
\end{equation}
These graph-specific settings are distinct from the environment observation
normalization and reward normalization, which remain enabled in every run.
